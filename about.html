About Me

Why I am studying Computer Engineering and where that path started.

Who I am

My name is David Oiwona Ejila. I am from Benue State. I am a final-year Computer Engineering student at Ahmadu Bello University, Zaria (registration number U21CO2032).

I finished a Diploma in Computer Engineering at Iya Abubakar in 2019. That diploma is what first pulled me into hardware, software and practical lab work. I later continued into the B.Eng programme at ABU so I could go deeper than the diploma allowed.

Why Computer Engineering

I want to become a computer engineer because I like systems that people can actually use. Class work on digital systems, programming and documentation is the main path I am on. My school projects follow that same line: medication tools for older people, a SIWES automation job, and a final year hardware plus software build.

I want to finish the degree with a clear understanding of how computers and electronics work together, then keep building useful tools.

Outside school

I invest in stocks and real estate. I also like to travel and go on vacations. I produce music as MarvelPro. Selected records and stream figures are on the Projects page. There are more records than the ones named there.
